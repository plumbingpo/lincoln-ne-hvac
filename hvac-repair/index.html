Warning Signs for HVAC Repair

Spotting the early warning signs gives you the chance to act before the situation spirals into something far more expensive. These are the signals our technicians see most often when a problem is underway:

  • Utility bills climbing while comfort keeps falling
  • Unfamiliar sounds from the equipment, like squealing, grinding, or banging
  • Weak airflow from the supply registers throughout the house
  • A humming outdoor unit that never kicks on
  • Frequent on-off cycling while the house never gets to the thermostat setting
  • A circuit breaker that keeps tripping when the system starts
  • Warm air from the vents in cooling mode, or cool air in heating mode
  • Water pooling around the indoor equipment, often from a clogged condensate drain

HVAC Repair Prevention Tips

Smart maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Change the filter every one to three months so the blower and coil never starve for air
  • Call about unusual noises early, since a squealing bearing costs far less to fix before the motor seizes
  • Clear the condensate drain every spring so algae never backs it up and shuts the system down
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of grass, leaves, and shrubs so the condenser coil can breathe
  • Ease the strain in extreme weather by nudging the thermostat a few degrees toward the outdoor temperature
  • Schedule professional maintenance twice a year, before cooling season and before heating season
  • Leave every supply and return vent open and free of furniture or rugs
