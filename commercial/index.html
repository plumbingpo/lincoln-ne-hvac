Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Lincoln HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the heat right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by service logs and written reports after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched quickly, throughout Lincoln and the surrounding area.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records and facility files.
